# ProofCheck

Check UPI payment screenshots against your bank statement, on your own laptop.

Tuition teachers, housing-society treasurers and home bakers collect payments by UPI and get a
screenshot on WhatsApp as "proof". Matching those screenshots against the bank statement is slow
by hand, and fake payment screenshots are a common scam. ProofCheck reads the screenshots with
local open-weight models, matches each UPI reference number against your statement, and tells you
who has actually paid.

Nothing is uploaded anywhere: the models run locally and your bank statement never leaves your
machine.
